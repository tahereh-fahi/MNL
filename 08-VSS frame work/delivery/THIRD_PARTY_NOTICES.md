# Third-party materials

**Author and maintainer:** Tahereh Fahi  
**Last updated:** September 21, 2026

Game artwork, sprites, HUD crops, OCR software, and model weights are not authored by the framework maintainer. Their original ownership and applicable terms remain separate from framework authorship. No new blanket software or artwork license is assigned by this delivery.

Weapon/passive icon manifests retain source URLs. Optional wiki gem references retain their source declaration. World-pickup references retain source URLs and hashes. Some legacy gem/HUD template crop lineage remains unresolved in the research audit. Local preparation of this package does not resolve redistribution permission.

Before public publication, the lab must resolve distribution of required reference assets and fixtures. If they cannot be bundled publicly, provide an authorized private asset bundle with exact hashes and installation instructions, and verify the complete installation using that bundle. Do not silently omit required resources from a supposedly runnable package.

Dependencies and model weights are obtained separately using their upstream distributions. Consult their original notices and terms. OCR weights, private recordings, and research run artifacts are excluded from source control.
